## Getting started

Access the Bahodir Brat Lottery Bot through Telegram to participate in live lottery draws during YouTube streams.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Telegram bot">
  Search for `@BahodirLotteryBot` in Telegram or use the direct link provided during live streams.
  
  <Check>
  Click "START" to begin registration
  </Check>
</Step>

<Step title="Automatic registration">
  The bot automatically registers you using your Telegram account information.
  
  ```
  ✅ Xush kelibsiz, [Your Name]!
  
  Telegram ID: 123456789
  Ro'yxatdan o'tish: 2024-01-15
  ```
  
  <Note>
  Your Telegram username and ID are used for authentication. No additional registration required.
  </Note>
</Step>

<Step title="Access Web App">
  Click "🎫 Bilet sotib olish" to open the lottery web application.
  
  The Web App opens directly in Telegram with full access to:
  - Current lottery draws
  - Ticket purchasing
  - Order history
  - Winner announcements
</Step>
</Steps>

## Purchasing tickets

### Step-by-step purchase process

<Steps>
<Step title="Select lottery draw">
  View active lottery draws in the Web App:
  
  ```
  🎬 Jonli efir: BAHODIR BRAT #45
  📅 Sana: 2024-01-20 20:00
  🎁 Sovg'alar: iPhone 15 Pro, AirPods Pro
  
  Bilet narxlari:
  • Oddiy: 500 RUB
  • Oltin: 1,000 RUB  
  • VIP: 1,500 RUB
  ```
  
  Click "Bilet sotib olish" to proceed.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose ticket tier">
  Select your preferred ticket type:
  
  <Tabs>
  <Tab title="Standard">
    - Most affordable option
    - 1x chance to win
    - Suitable for casual participants
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Gold">
    - 2x better odds than Standard
    - Moderate investment
    - Popular choice
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="VIP">
    - 3x better odds than Standard
    - Maximum winning probability
    - For serious participants
  </Tab>
  </Tabs>
</Step>

<Step title="Select quantity">
  Choose how many tickets to purchase:
  
  ```
  Bilet turi: VIP (1,500 RUB)
  Miqdor: [1] [5] [10] [Custom]
  
  Jami: 15,000 RUB (10 bilet)
  ```
  
  <Tip>
  Buying multiple tickets increases your total winning chances. Each ticket participates independently in the draw.
  </Tip>
</Step>

<Step title="Review order">
  Confirm your order details:
  
  ```
  📋 BUYURTMA TAFSILOTLARI
  
  Lotereya: BAHODIR BRAT #45
  Bilet turi: VIP
  Miqdor: 10 ta
  Narx: 1,500 RUB × 10
  
  JAMI: 15,000 RUB
  ```
  
  Click "Tasdiqlash" to create the order.
</Step>

<Step title="Get payment details">
  Receive payment instructions:
  
  ```
  💳 TO'LOV MA'LUMOTLARI
  
  Karta raqami: 2202 2063 XXXX XXXX
  Bank: Sberbank
  Summa: 15,000 RUB
  Buyurtma: #12345
  
  ⚠️ MUHIM:
  1. Aynan 15,000 RUB o'tkazing
  2. To'lovdan keyin chek rasmi yuboring
  3. Chekda karta raqami ko'rinishi shart
  ```
  
  <Warning>
  Transfer the EXACT amount shown. Incorrect amounts will delay verification.
  </Warning>
</Step>
</Steps>

### Uploading payment receipt

After making the payment, upload your receipt for verification.

<Steps>
<Step title="Take receipt photo">
  Capture a clear photo of your payment receipt showing:
  
  <AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Required information">
    ✅ **Must be visible:**
    - Card number (last 4 digits minimum)
    - Transfer amount (exact match)
    - Date and time
    - Transaction ID
    - Recipient details
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Photo requirements">
    - Format: JPG or PNG
    - Maximum size: 5MB
    - Clear and readable
    - No blur or glare
    - All corners visible
  </Accordion>
  </AccordionGroup>
</Step>

<Step title="Upload to bot">
  In the Web App, go to your order and click "Chek yuklash":
  
  ```
  📸 CHEK YUKLASH
  
  Buyurtma: #12345
  Summa: 15,000 RUB
  
  [Rasmni tanlang] [Kameradan olish]
  ```
  
  Select your receipt photo and upload.
  
  <Check>
  You'll see confirmation: "Chek muvaffaqiyatli yuklandi. Tekshirilmoqda..."
  </Check>
</Step>

<Step title="Wait for verification">
  Admin will verify your payment within 1-24 hours:
  
  ```
  ⏳ Tekshirilmoqda...
  
  Buyurtma: #12345
  Yuklangan: 2024-01-15 14:30
  Status: Kutilmoqda
  
  Admin tez orada tekshiradi.
  ```
  
  <Note>
  Verification is typically faster during business hours (10:00-22:00 Moscow time).
  </Note>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Payment not verified after 24 hours">
  **Solution:**
  1. Check if receipt photo is clear and complete
  2. Verify you transferred the exact amount
  3. Contact @admin with your order number
  4. Provide transaction ID for faster resolution
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can't upload receipt photo">
  **Solution:**
  1. Check file size (must be under 5MB)
  2. Use JPG or PNG format only
  3. Try compressing the image
  4. Take a new photo with better lighting
  5. Contact support if issue persists
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Wrong amount transferred">
  **Solution:**
  1. Contact @admin immediately
  2. Provide transaction details
  3. Admin will guide you on next steps
  4. May need to transfer difference or request refund
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Didn't receive tickets after approval">
  **Solution:**
  1. Check "Faol biletlarim" section
  2. Refresh the Web App
  3. Contact @admin with order number
  4. Tickets should appear within 5 minutes of approval
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
